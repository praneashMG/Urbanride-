Our Story

Redefining Urban Mobility

Born from a passion for cycling and a love for our city, we are on a mission to make exploring the urban landscape greener, healthier, and more accessible.

The Mission

Moving People Forward

UrbanRide was founded in 2015 by two cycling enthusiasts who believed that the best way to experience a city isn't through the tinted windows of a tour bus, but from the saddle of a bicycle.

What started as a modest fleet of 10 cruiser bikes in a small downtown garage has evolved into the city's premier micro-mobility and guided tour network. Our vision is simple: reduce urban carbon emissions, promote active lifestyles, and connect people with the hidden gems of their neighborhoods.

Eco-Conscious Promise

100% of our hubs are powered by renewable energy, and our maintenance fleet is entirely electric.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Uncompromising Safety

We never cut corners on maintenance. Every bike in our fleet undergoes rigorous daily inspections to ensure a flawless ride.

Community First

We hire locally, partner with independent cafes for our tours, and actively advocate for safer city biking infrastructure.

Sustainable Future

By replacing car trips with bike rides, we are actively fighting urban congestion and reducing our collective carbon footprint.

Spirit of Adventure

We believe every ride should be an experience. We build routes that encourage discovery, joy, and a sense of wonder.

Our Milestones

The Road So Far

2015

The Beginning

UrbanRide opens its first modest garage hub downtown with a fleet of just 10 refurbished cruiser bikes.

2018

Electric Revolution

We introduced our first batch of pedal-assist E-Bikes, making the city's steep coastal hills accessible to all fitness levels.

2021

City-Wide Network

Expanded our operations to 25 automated hubs and launched our proprietary mobile booking application.

2026

The Present

Today, we manage over 50+ locations, 5 full-service flagship stores, and host over 10,000 guided tours annually.
