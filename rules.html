Safe & Smart Riding

Rules of the Road

To ensure a safe, enjoyable experience for you and the community, please review our rental policies and local riding regulations before you pedal.

Safety First

Mandatory Safety Guidelines

Helmets Required

By local law and our policy, all riders must wear a securely fastened helmet at all times while the bicycle is in motion.

Obey Traffic Laws

Bicycles are considered vehicles. You must stop at red lights, yield at stop signs, and ride in the direction of traffic.

Yield to Pedestrians

Pedestrians always have the right of way. Stay off crowded sidewalks and use designated bike lanes whenever available.

No Distracted Riding

Do not use your phone while riding. Use the provided handlebar mount for navigation and pull over if you need to text or call.

Financials

Deposits & Fees

To protect our fleet, we require a temporary security hold on all rentals. This is not a charge, but an authorization that is released upon the safe return of the equipment.

Accepted Methods

Major Credit Cards, Debit Cards (Hold times may vary by bank), Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

$50

Standard Bike Deposit

A $50 authorization hold is placed on your card for all Standard Cruisers and Mountain Bikes. The hold is instantly voided when the bike is securely locked at a hub.

$200

E-Bike & Specialty Deposit

Due to the higher value of Electric Bikes and Cargo attachments, a $200 hold is required per vehicle. Funds usually release within 1-3 business days depending on your bank.

Late Return Penalties

Bikes returned more than 30 minutes past the scheduled end time will be billed an automatic $10 late fee, plus the standard hourly rate for every additional hour.

Rider Responsibilities

The Do's & Don'ts

Please Do

  • Lock the bike securely to a designated rack or solid immovable object whenever left unattended.
  • Report maintenance issues immediately through the app if you notice flat tires, loose brakes, or chain issues.
  • Use hand signals to indicate turns or stops to vehicles behind you.
  • Stay visible by wearing light colors and keeping the automatic lights clear of bags or jackets at night.

Please Don't

  • Ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs. It is illegal and highly dangerous.
  • Carry additional passengers unless using a specifically designated cargo bike or child seat add-on.
  • Ride on highways or roads with speed limits exceeding 40mph unless there is a separated bike path.
  • Sub-lease the bicycle to another person. The renter is solely responsible for the equipment.

Liability & Insurance

Damage & Theft Policy

As the renter, you are responsible for the bicycle from the moment it is unlocked until it is securely docked. If a bicycle is stolen or severely damaged while under your account, your card will be charged for the replacement value.

Optional Damage Waiver

For $5/day, our Damage Waiver covers accidental cosmetic damage (scratches, bent spokes, flat tires). Note: This waiver does NOT cover theft or loss of the bicycle.
